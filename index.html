Mobility

We set up a mobile batch plant on the job site and produce concrete on demand. Every load travels minutes from plant to pour, so the 90-minute batch-to-discharge window never comes into play. This is how it works:

  • Mobilize: Our trucks bring the plant and crew. It sets up on hydraulic outriggers, with no foundation, in about an acre.
  • Pour: Our crew runs the plant on your schedule, with batch records for every load and QC testing tailored to your project.
  • Move On: When the job’s done, we break down and leave no permanent footprint.

Scalability

The buildout is national, and our reach follows it. Hub-and-spoke material sourcing keeps each plant fed wherever the job lands, so the only limit on growth is the size of our fleet.

  • Hub-and-spoke cement, SCM and aggregate supply
  • Redundant suppliers secured ahead of demand
  • Two pours of material staged on site before batching starts
  • Up to 196 CY/hr rated output per plant
